# Security Policy for Tours & Travels

## Reporting Security Issues

If you discover a security vulnerability in this website, please email us at:
**security@your-domain.com**

Please do NOT:
- Publicly disclose the vulnerability before we have had a chance to fix it
- Access more data than necessary to confirm the vulnerability
- Use the vulnerability to access systems or data belonging to others
- Perform testing that could degrade or disrupt service availability

## What We Promise

- We will respond to security reports within 48 hours
- We will work to fix confirmed vulnerabilities promptly
- We will acknowledge your responsible disclosure (if desired)
- We will keep you informed of the fix timeline

## Scope

This policy applies to:
- https://your-domain.com
- All subdomains under your-domain.com

Out of scope:
- Third-party services and websites
- Infrastructure not controlled by Tours & Travels

## Vulnerability Disclosure Timeline

- Day 1: We acknowledge receipt of your report
- Day 1-7: We investigate and confirm the vulnerability
- Day 7-30: We develop and test a fix
- Day 30+: We deploy the fix and notify you
